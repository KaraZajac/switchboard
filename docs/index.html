Three ways to run it

The same program, the same login, the same conversation — wherever you happen to be

💻

On your desktop

The full client for macOS, Linux and Windows. It holds the connections, keeps the history, and hands over to your phone when you close the lid.

📱

On your phone

An Android client with its own IRC engine — not a remote control. Pair it with a QR code and it takes over the connections when the desktop goes away, then gives them back.

🌐

On a box that never sleeps

A headless build with no Electron and no window, running on a server or a Pi. It holds your connections around the clock and speaks IRC to your other clients, so it is your bouncer as well as your client.

Devices find each other over an encrypted peer-to-peer link: no ports to open, and nothing of ours in the middle. They hole-punch straight to each other where the network allows it, and fall back to a public relay that only ever carries bytes it cannot read. Your networks and passwords travel between them in a vault sealed with one passphrase.

Features

Everything you need from an IRC client, with a modern twist

🌐

Multi-Server

Connect to as many IRC networks as you want, all at once. Each server gets its own icon in the sidebar.

💬

Rich Messaging

Replies, reactions, typing indicators, read markers, message editing, deletion, and full IRC color/formatting support.

🎨

GIF Picker

Search and send GIFs, stickers, clips, memes, and emoji via Klipy — right from the message composer.

📎

File Uploads

Upload files via draft/filehost. Images and video render inline; other files get a download card.

🔗

Link Previews

OpenGraph metadata cards for shared URLs with title, description, and thumbnail — fetched automatically.

👥

Friends

One list across every network, written nick@network, so “is anybody about?” is one glance rather than a tour of your networks. Uses MONITOR, or WATCH on the networks that only speak that.

👤

User Profiles

Edit your nickname, realname, and avatar. View other users' WHOIS info with a click.

📅

Chat History

Messages stored locally in SQLite and fetched from the server via draft/chathistory. Never miss a conversation.

🔎

Search everywhere

Full-text search across every network at once — because what you remember is a phrase or a person, not which network they said it on. Quick channel switcher with Ctrl+K.

👉

Mentions

Every line that named you, from every network, newest first. A badge tells you something happened; this tells you what was said.

🔗

Go to the message

A mention, a search result or a reply quote takes you to the line itself, with what was being said around it — fetching the conversation from the server if it is older than anything held locally.

📡

Works with your bouncer

Speaks soju.im/bouncer-networks to soju, and recognises ZNC — including the user/network login you already have. A bouncer holding five networks becomes five networks here, offered rather than hidden.

🔒

Encrypted at rest

Every message, network and password in an encrypted SQLite database, with the key in your OS keychain. On Android, the device identity is sealed by the hardware keystore and kept out of backups.

🔇

Muting

Timed or permanent mute for channels and servers. No more notification spam from noisy channels.

🎨

13 Themes

Catppuccin, Dracula, Nord, Gruvbox, One Dark, Rosé Pine, Solarized, Tokyo Night, Kanagawa, Discord, and more.

💻

Cross-Platform

macOS (.dmg), Linux (.AppImage, .deb, .rpm) and Windows (.exe) with auto-updates, plus an Android APK per architecture.
